    Exercise for Kids

    Regular physical activity is important for children's health and development. Encourage your kids to be active every day!

    • Play outside
    • Join a sports team
    • Take family walks or bike rides

    Sleep

    Sleep for Kids

    Getting enough sleep is crucial for children's growth and learning. Make sure your kids have a consistent bedtime routine.

    • Set a regular bedtime
    • Create a calm sleep environment
    • Limit screen time before bed
