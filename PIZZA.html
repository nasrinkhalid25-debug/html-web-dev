Pizza

Pizza is a savory dish of Italian origin consisting of a usually round, flattened base of leavened wheat dough topped with tomato sauce, cheese, and various other ingredients such as anchovies, mushrooms, onions, olives, vegetables, meat, and herbs. The dish is then baked in a hot oven. Pizza is a popular food in many countries, and variants are available worldwide.
